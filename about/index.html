Who We Are

A regional platform for athletes, families, teams, and communities.

Heartland Athletics Group LLC was created to bring more organized, accessible, and meaningful athletic opportunities to the Heartland region.

H.A.G. is not limited to one sport, one team, one season, or one type of event. We are building a year-round, multi-sport network centered on athlete development, competition, leadership, community partnerships, and local recognition.

Our work is designed to connect athletes, families, coaches, facilities, businesses, and communities through quality athletic experiences.

Our Direction

Mission and vision.

These statements guide how H.A.G. evaluates opportunities, partnerships, programs, and long-term growth.

Our Mission

Create exceptional athletic experiences that strengthen athletes, families, and communities through competition, development, and leadership.

Our Vision

Become the Midwest’s premier youth athletics organization for tournaments, leagues, camps, clinics, and community sporting events.

Our Why

Inspired by family and a passion for youth sports.

Heartland Athletics Group was inspired by our own family’s experience in youth sports.

As parents of children heavily involved in area athletics, we have seen firsthand how valuable sports can be for confidence, discipline, development, leadership, teamwork, and community.

We have also seen the need for more opportunities, stronger connections, and quality experiences for athletes and families across the region. H.A.G. was created to help meet that need.

Heartland Athletics Group is bigger than one team, one sport, or one season. It is about building something that serves families, supports local athletes, and strengthens the sports community throughout our area.

Founded by Justin Hawkins and inspired by family, community, and a passion for youth sports.
