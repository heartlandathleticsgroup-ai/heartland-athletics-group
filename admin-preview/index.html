✦ AI News Writing Assistant

Write H.A.G. news with a real editorial angle—not generic announcements. Supply what happened, why it matters, and what readers should know next. The resulting draft always requires your review.

A story angle sets the theme; it does not verify history. For example, a 31-year tradition or the year a tournament last took place must be included here as verified historical background before the AI can assert it. If you have not verified it, leave it out of published copy.

Tip: The AI now checks for duplicate summary/body copy and attempts to remove it before showing a brief. Put historical facts in Verified history, not just Why this story matters. New facts—not extra adjectives—make an article newsworthy.

Only administrator-approved facts from this topic are sent to the AI; draft or private records are excluded. Go to Fact Library to add or approve information.

Choose News brief when you have a date, planning update, or one main development. Use Full article only when you have several specific verified facts, approved quotes, or a meaningful background story. The assistant will not manufacture substance.

AI drafts may make mistakes. Your submitted focal points are sent to GroqCloud only when you request a draft. Do not submit private information about minors. Verify facts, quotes, and permissions before approving. Up to 20 drafts per staff member every 24 hours. Groq free-plan usage limits apply; the service must be activated with a server-side key. Nothing publishes automatically.

AI assistant activation

The AI drafting backend now uses GroqCloud GPT-OSS 120B with verified staff access and a 20-draft daily limit. A Groq free-plan API key is needed before it can generate articles.

In your H.A.G. Supabase project, choose Edge Functions → Secrets and add a secret named GROQ_API_KEY with a key from your GroqCloud account. Keys must only be saved in Supabase Secrets—not in this website, in your article notes, in GitHub, or in chat.

GroqCloud Free Plan usage is free within its rate limits. Check your Groq plan before enabling paid billing. The AI assistant can create drafts but cannot approve or publish them.
