Need account recovery?

Use a previously enrolled authenticator or a backed-up Apple Passwords verification code. If unavailable, contact the authorized H.A.G. Supabase organization owner through a known private channel. The owner must verify identity, disable access and revoke compromised sessions or factors before re-enrollment. Never send passwords, setup keys or codes by email or text.

There is no self-service MFA bypass or public password-reset form.
