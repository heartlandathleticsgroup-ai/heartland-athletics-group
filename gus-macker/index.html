It's Official • Memorial Day Weekend 2027

Gus Macker - Quincy

May 29–30, 2027 • Quincy, Illinois

The Gus Macker 3-on-3 basketball tournament returns to Quincy during Memorial Day weekend in 2027. Brought to you by Heartland Athletics Group. The tournament's return and dates are confirmed. The venue and additional event details will be announced once agreements are finalized.

Businesses, families, former players, and community supporters can help make Memorial Day weekend 2027 a great event for Quincy.
