About H.A.G.

A regional platform for athletes, families, teams, and communities.

Heartland Athletics Group LLC was created to bring more organized, accessible, and meaningful athletic opportunities to the Heartland region.

H.A.G. is not limited to one sport, one team, one season, or one type of event. We are building a year-round, multi-sport network centered on athlete development, competition, leadership, community partnerships, and local recognition.

Featured H.A.G. Program

Heartland Enforcers Basketball Club

One Club. One Standard.

Heartland Enforcers is a development-focused basketball club for boys and girls. The club is built around purposeful instruction, competitive opportunities, accountable coaching, and a team culture centered on effort, discipline, teamwork, sportsmanship, preparation, and respect.

Club message: We don’t enforce people. We enforce the standard.
