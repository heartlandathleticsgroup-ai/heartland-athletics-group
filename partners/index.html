Community Partnership

Help us build stronger athletic opportunities.

Heartland Athletics Group works with businesses, organizations, facilities, volunteers, and community supporters who believe in creating quality athletic experiences for athletes and families.

Why Partnerships Matter

Local support creates meaningful regional impact.

Community partnerships help H.A.G. create organized, affordable, and developmental athletic opportunities across multiple sports and seasons.

Support may help make facility rentals, insurance, equipment, staffing, awards, apparel, event operations, marketing, athlete recognition, and future programming possible.

Each partnership can be structured around the goals, resources, and level of involvement that make sense for the supporting business or organization.

Request Partnership Information

Partnership Support May Include

  • Financial sponsorship
  • Facility or venue access
  • Equipment and supplies
  • Food, beverages, and hospitality
  • Printing and promotional support
  • Volunteer service and event staffing
  • Professional services
  • Community outreach and promotion

H.A.G. welcomes both financial and in-kind partnership conversations.

Facility & Venue Partnerships

Have space that could support athletic opportunities?

H.A.G. is interested in connecting with gyms, schools, churches, community centers, training facilities, athletic complexes, outdoor fields, meeting spaces, and other suitable venues.

Facility partnerships may support practices, camps, clinics, tournaments, small-sided competitions, meetings, leadership sessions, training programs, and community events.

Partnerships may involve traditional rentals, discounted access, in-kind use, shared programming, recurring arrangements, or other mutually beneficial structures.

Partnership Process

Start with a conversation.

Prospective partners can submit an inquiry describing their organization, the type of support they are considering, and any specific program, event, or community goal they would like to discuss.

H.A.G. will review the inquiry, evaluate alignment and operational needs, and follow up regarding appropriate next steps.

Recognition

Partnerships should create mutual value.

Depending on the program, event, and level of support, partner recognition may include website placement, social media recognition, event materials, signage, announcements, logo placement, or other agreed benefits.

Recognition opportunities are determined by the specific partnership and the available program or event assets.
