## Templates and isolated outbox
Plain-text and minimal HTML confirmation, preference-access and requested unsubscribe messages. No marketing content, tracking pixels, external images, or live URLs; fixture destinations and preview.invalid links only, token in URL fragment. Confirmation requires explicit action, never state-changing GET. Expiration stated from issuance. Final sender, reply-to, business postal address, privacy policy, domain authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), disabled click/open tracking and deliverability review require approval. FTC guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business . Per-message campaign opt-out must remain available 30 days after actual send and meet other applicable rules; 60-day requested unsubscribe capability is not yet a campaign unsubscribe mechanism.
Staged runtime now uses bounded in-memory email simulation with 200 jobs, 256-bit random AES-GCM key, HMAC job and destination/purpose identifiers, no token or recipient in status summaries. Fixture-only synthetic acceptance, never actual email or provider call. Same-token retries use one stable job ID. Tests simulate uncertain acknowledgement, repeated requests, transient and permanent failures. 3 attempts, bounded backoff, 15-minute job deadline from pre-issuance time, no delivery if a scoped capability is invalid, expired, already used or suppressed for confirmation. Manage and unsubscribe remain available on suppressed channels. Terminal encrypted payload cleared, metadata purged after 24 hours when maintenance called; close clears keys and memory. JavaScript memory clearing is best effort, not guaranteed erasure. Accepted means synthetic provider accepted, not actual delivery. No bounce/open/delivered claim.
This is not a durable transactional outbox: a process restart or crash after DB issue can lose a job, and a recheck can race revocation. The simulated provider accepts once within the process, not a guarantee of exactly-once real delivery. Before launch implement atomic durable outbox, encrypted-at-rest secrets with rotation, leases and stale-worker fencing, provider idempotency and ambiguous outcome reconciliation, verified delivery webhook and suppression synchronization. No raw tokens or addresses in logs. No production driver or provider credentials.
## Retention
Move unbounded lazy deletes out of request transactions; private maintenance role can remove at most 500 expired capability, old abuse, and old request-key rows per call per table. Live capabilities are retained; threshold one day past expiration or window/creation timestamp. Separate role cannot operate public subscription flows. No consent, contacts, subscriptions or suppression deletes. No job scheduled or installed; prior pending-contact retention proposal is unresolved and requires approved deletion policy and FK-safe cleanup. Test in disposable PostgreSQL only. Monitor backlog and schedule approved recurring maintenance before public traffic. Supabase connection and pool guidance: https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/connecting-to-postgres . Production TLS, actual driver acquisition/statement deadlines, narrowly scoped login and separate pools still require integrated testing.
