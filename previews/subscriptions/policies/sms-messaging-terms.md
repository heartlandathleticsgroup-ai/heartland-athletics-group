# Heartland Athletics Group LLC SMS Messaging Terms
Proposed effective date: [upon approved SMS launch]

## Program
Heartland Athletics Group LLC, serving the Quincy, Illinois and Tri-State region, will offer optional recurring automated marketing text messages to adults who expressly opt in and verify their mobile number. Messages may include selected H.A.G. news, Heartland Enforcers updates and tryouts, Gus Macker – Quincy event and registration updates, camps and clinics, tournaments, sponsorship and volunteer opportunities. SMS is separate from email; no inquiry or registration automatically enrolls you.

## Consent
By affirmatively selecting the SMS consent box, providing a mobile number you are authorized to use and completing verification, you agree to receive recurring automated marketing text messages from Heartland Athletics Group LLC about the sports and update types you select for SMS. Sport-specific marketing matches both interests. Selecting all current and future sports includes later-added sports; otherwise later-added sports are not automatically selected. Adding text interests requires renewed consent and confirmation when supported. Consent is not required to purchase, register or participate. You must be at least 18 years old. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consult your wireless plan for applicable charges.

## Opt-out
Reply STOP to a message to unsubscribe from all H.A.G. marketing SMS. We will also honor recognized commands including QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL and UNSUBSCRIBE, and other reasonable revocation requests through our monitored contact channels. A nonmarketing confirmation of your opt-out may be sent where lawful. You may use the unsubscribe link or contact us directly. Opt-outs are honored promptly; no purchase, fee, login or new marketing consent is required. Sport and update-type preferences do not override a channel-wide opt-out. Email preferences are separate. Rejoining requires a separately approved, verified re-consent process; it will not occur automatically.

## Privacy
See H.A.G.'s Privacy Policy (/privacy/). We do not sell or share mobile opt-in information or consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Necessary service providers may process information on our behalf to deliver and administer messages. Verification, consent and suppression records are maintained to support your choices.

## Changes
We may update these terms and the program, with notice as required. Any materially expanded marketing consent requires a new affirmative opt-in. No provider, sending number or SMS program has been activated as of this review.
