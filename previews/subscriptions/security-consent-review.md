## Review scope and corrections
Reviewed the staged HTML/CSS/JS, shared consent and request envelopes, server-only gateway/adapter, synthetic database and delivery, SQL entrypoints, grants and tests, and exact-commit workflows. Phase 3C.3D Chromium acceptance passed at five viewport widths, including labelled controls, focus, touch target and full synthetic flow. The PR does not alter production routes, Formspree or authentication. No live service.
- Fixed misleading successful no-op confirmations; a confirmation must activate at least one eligible pending topic. Added non-consuming, rate-limited scope inspection using the existing token hash and immutable snapshot, filtered against active topics, recorded consent and suppressions. The UI displays scoped topics and consent before the explicit confirmation action. No destination or unrelated data returned. The scope can narrow before redemption; no new topics can be activated by the old token. New consent is reset when selections change.
- Allow withdrawal of a retired subscription topic, and withdrawal-only edits without accepting a newer marketing consent version. Preserve confirmation links for unrelated pending topics when another preference changes; invalidate intersecting snapshots and prior edit/manage capabilities.
- Separate bounded opt-out ingress lane (20/source/minute, 120/process/minute) from ordinary requests (40/source/minute, 600/process/minute). Only unsubscribe POST accepted on that lane. Prior database source budget is separate. Still possible to exhaust the opt-out lane; production fallback/monitoring needed.
- Raw delivery tokens never belong in public issue responses, logs, analytics, URLs sent to servers, or static assets. The browser test inbox and synthetic adapters must not ship. A verified edit token stays in memory, scoped, single-use and short-lived. Public capability URLs should use a fragment, remove it from history immediately, no-referrer/no-cache, no third-party scripts, explicit POST, and server-side rate limit; implement and test in actual runtime. Email link scanning must not change state through GET.
- Fixed parameters and service-only grants protect private SQL access. Production must use a narrowly scoped runtime database identity and restricted connection, not an unrestricted service role handed to browser code. No schema installation or public RPC wrapper authorized. Verify no default privileges expose new tables/functions. Production administrator auth unchanged.

Email: CAN-SPAM requires accurate sender/header/subject, advertising identification when required, valid postal address, conspicuous no-cost opt-out; process requests within ten business days and maintain the mechanism for at least 30 days after each send. No extra verification/login, fee or more than one page/reply step may be required. HAG elects stricter affirmative double opt-in as an organizational policy, not a universal CAN-SPAM requirement. Current 60-day token begins at issuance, NOT dispatch. Before first campaign, mint per-message unsubscribe links with adequate lifespan at actual dispatch and verify direct one-page opt-out plus alternative monitored route; do not force recipient to request a new confirmation. Verify provider one-click List-Unsubscribe support, suppression recheck immediately before delivery and no marketing in verification messages. Owner must provide valid business mailing address (a qualifying PO box/private mailbox may be used). Do not publish a personal home address or buy a mailbox.
SMS: For applicable automated telemarketing obtain prior express written consent, identified seller, authorized number, clear electronic affirmative act, automated marketing disclosure and no purchase condition. Current checkbox is separate and optional. Adopt full-channel marketing suppression rather than topic-only STOP; the new FCC rule allows informational category-specific revocation in certain circumstances and designated disclosed opt-out methods; marketing revocations cover all future marketing calls/texts from the sender. Design to recognize STOP, QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL and UNSUBSCRIBE, honor reasonable requests through monitored contact routes too, process promptly (legal outer limit when applicable ten business days). HELP, signed/replay-safe webhook, direct carrier suppression sync, reassigned-number and applicable do-not-call requirements, provider registration and messaging terms are NOT yet implemented/verified. No provider selected or activated. Review state telemarketing applicability and any recipient jurisdiction before sending.
Youth: HAG serves youth sports; adult-only marketing is deliberate, not a blanket COPPA exemption. Adult attestation alone does not resolve child-directed/mixed-audience or actual-knowledge obligations. Review audience, age screening before collection, no direct child marketing, known-underage requests, deletion and separate parental/guardian workflows with counsel. Keep player/guardian records separate from marketing, no automatic opt-in from inquiries, registrations or third-party lists.
Privacy: reasonable safeguards and incident procedures under applicable Illinois PIPA (statutory personal-information definitions matter); review other states as audience expands. Disclose purpose, categories, processors, contact, rights, retention, security limitations and changes. Do not share/sell mobile opt-in for other parties' marketing. Document vendor agreements and breach response. No data beyond needed contact method, preferences, consent/version/time/source and bounded abuse metadata; name is currently required in draft, review whether necessary. No real minors' data in tests.
