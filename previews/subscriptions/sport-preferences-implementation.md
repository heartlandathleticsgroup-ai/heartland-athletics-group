## Legal and security review (2026-11-27)
- CAN-SPAM: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business — accurate sender, subject, valid physical postal address, clear opt-out and timely honoring (within ten business days), no fee or login. Existing welcome and unsubscribe capability preserved. Future campaign delivery must include full footer and opt-out, recheck suppression and eligibility at dispatch, and be independently tested. Sender/provider identity and postal footer must be verified privately.
- FCC/TCPA: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-64/subpart-L/section-64.1200 — affirmative channel-specific consent, frequency/rates disclosure, reasonable revocation handling, and no condition of purchase. SMS remains disabled; provider-specific confirmation, STOP/HELP, carrier registration, reassigned-number policy, applicable state rules and actual costs require review before activation. The isolated confirmation core is not a provider integration.
- COPPA: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions — adult-only signup is a safeguard, not a blanket exemption for a youth-sports website. Review actual audience classification, children’s data and any separate registrations, retention/deletion, processing arrangements, contact monitoring, and effective policy versions before publishing. No legal certification is claimed.
- Security: private tables with forced RLS, explicit privilege revocations, authenticated admin wrapper checks the existing recent proof, no browser table grants, fixed parameterized gateway SQL, hashed one-use capabilities, and no public raw SMS token. Campaign drafts are not send jobs. Before production: verify exact deployed package, role grants, hosted CORS and rate limiting, mobile accessibility, opt-out availability during provider outage, and real provider identity and configuration.
