Privacy Policy

Heartland Athletics Group LLC (H.A.G.) operates heartlandathleticsgroup.com and provides information about programs, events, partnerships and youth athletics in the Quincy and Tri-State area.

Information and purposes

Our contact form transmits your name, email, optional phone, inquiry category and message using Formspree. We use submissions to respond and administer your request. An inquiry is not marketing consent. Our site uses GitHub Pages hosting and Supabase for administration and public content. These services may process device, connection and request information in operating and securing their services. Third-party registration and payment services have their own terms and privacy notices.

When email subscriptions are enabled, we collect the adult subscriber's name, email, chosen topics, consent wording and version, time, source, preference changes and opt-outs. Limited technical and keyed rate-limit identifiers may be used to prevent abuse. Email signup is single opt-in and does not establish address ownership. We do not send a signup confirmation or welcome email. Text enrollment is not currently available; no phone number is collected by the subscription form.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information or share it with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in information and consent are not sold, rented or shared for another party's marketing. Service providers may process necessary information solely on our behalf. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect rights, safety or security. Future messaging providers will be identified before activation.

Choices, retention and safety

Subscriptions are optional and do not affect registration or eligibility. Contact info@heartlandathleticsgroup.com to withdraw consent or request access, correction or deletion. We may verify identity for privacy access or deletion, but not impose additional verification for a valid opt-out. Once delivery is enabled, marketing email will include a working unsubscribe link, and text messages will support STOP and HELP. We retain information as reasonably necessary to operate the service, document consent, honor suppression and satisfy legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards and respond to security incidents as required by applicable law.

Children and families

Our marketing subscriptions are for adults 18 or older. Do not submit a child's contact details. Parents and guardians should manage youth program communications and registrations. If we learn we collected information from a child under 13 without required consent, we will take appropriate action under applicable law. Contact us about any concern. Program waivers, registration notices and media permissions are separate.

Contact and updates

Heartland Athletics Group LLC · info@heartlandathleticsgroup.com · Contact us. Material policy changes will be posted with an effective date and further notice or consent when required. Changes do not automatically expand marketing consent.
