Heartland Athletics Group LLC Privacy Policy

Review draft — not yet effective. Subscription messaging is not activated.

Who we are

Heartland Athletics Group LLC (H.A.G., we, us) operates heartlandathleticsgroup.com and serves the Quincy, Illinois and Tri-State region, including information about Heartland Enforcers Basketball Club, Gus Macker – Quincy, camps, clinics, tournaments and related opportunities. This policy covers information collected through our website and proposed email and SMS subscription program. Separate registrations, waivers, third-party websites and future apps may have additional notices.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we receive the name, email address, phone number, selected inquiry category and message you provide. Our existing inquiry form uses Formspree to transmit submissions. An inquiry is not consent to marketing.

When our subscription program is launched, we will collect the contact method you choose, name if required, adult eligibility declaration, selected topics, confirmation and consent records (including wording, version, source and time), preference changes and opt-outs. Email and SMS are independent choices. Eligible email subscriptions begin upon affirmative website consent without a confirmation step; one automatic welcome acknowledgment may be sent with preference and unsubscribe links. this does not verify ownership of the address. SMS requires mobile verification. Secure preference access is verified when requested. We may process limited IP-derived rate-limit identifiers and technical request metadata for security and abuse prevention. Verification links use expiring credentials. We do not need payment card details, medical information, school records or a child's personal details for a marketing subscription.

Website hosting and service providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device information and request logs.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, organize programs and partnerships, provide requested updates, verify communication preferences, maintain consent and unsubscribe records, secure systems, prevent abuse, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. Marketing subscriptions are optional and are not a condition of registering, purchasing, volunteering or participating. We do not automatically subscribe people based on inquiries or event registrations.

Sharing and service providers

We use service providers to operate our website, receive inquiries, securely store information and, after activation, deliver requested communications. Providers receive information needed to perform contracted services. Existing planned administration storage is Supabase; Google Workspace is the preferred proposed email delivery option, not yet authorized for website sending; Formspree continues to process website inquiries.

We do not sell personal information or share it for another company's marketing. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in information and consent will not be sold, rented or shared with third parties for their own purposes. Contracted processors may handle necessary information solely to operate our messaging service. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety or security, subject to applicable law.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing email using the link in an email or by contacting us. When SMS is active, reply STOP or use the disclosed unsubscribe method; reply HELP for assistance. You may separately manage topics or withdraw a channel. We will honor reasonable revocation requests and maintain necessary suppression records to avoid sending unwanted messages. Opting out of marketing does not prevent responses you request or necessary nonmarketing communications where lawful. We do not require a purchase, fee or login to unsubscribe.

You may contact us to request access, correction, deletion or other applicable privacy rights. We may verify identity when necessary for privacy requests, but not require additional verification for a valid marketing opt-out. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, dispute or suppression purposes.

Children and families

Our marketing subscription program is intended for adults age 18 or older. Parents and guardians should submit inquiries and registrations involving minors. Please do not submit a child's contact details in marketing forms. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the marketing program. If we learn that such information was collected without required parental consent, we will take appropriate steps to delete it or address it under applicable law. Contact us with any concerns.

Retention and security

We retain information only as reasonably necessary for the described purposes, including responding to inquiries, managing active subscriptions, recording consent and honoring opt-outs. We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the information. No system can guarantee absolute security. We will maintain and apply a documented retention and disposal schedule and respond to security incidents as required by law.

External sites and changes

Links to national event organizers, sponsors, registration services and other sites are subject to their own policies. We may update this policy as practices or legal requirements change, posting the new effective date and providing additional notice or consent where required. We will not use a policy update to silently expand marketing consent.
