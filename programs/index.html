Featured H.A.G. Program

Heartland Enforcers Basketball Club

One Club. One Standard.

Heartland Enforcers is a development-focused basketball club for boys and girls. The program is built around purposeful instruction, competitive opportunities, accountable coaching, and a team culture centered on effort, discipline, teamwork, sportsmanship, preparation, and respect.

Club message: We don’t enforce people. We enforce the standard.

How Programs Develop

Built responsibly through interest and partnership.

H.A.G. evaluates new programs based on community need, athlete and family interest, qualified leadership, suitable facilities, insurance and safety requirements, financial sustainability, and the ability to provide a quality experience.

Not every opportunity listed on this page is active at all times. Some programs are established, while others remain in development until the proper resources and partnerships are in place.

Tell Us What Interests You Become a Partner

Program Readiness Includes

  • Demonstrated athlete and family interest
  • Qualified coaches, trainers, or event leadership
  • Appropriate facilities and equipment
  • Insurance, safety, and operating procedures
  • Community and sponsor support
  • A sustainable financial and operational plan

H.A.G. prioritizes quality, safety, communication, and long-term sustainability over launching programs before they are ready.
