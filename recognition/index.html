Celebrating people who represent the Heartland the right way.

Heartland Athletics Group recognizes athletes, teams, coaches, programs, volunteers, and community supporters who demonstrate growth, leadership, sportsmanship, commitment, and positive impact.

Why Recognition Matters

Every achievement has a story behind it.

Success in sports is not limited to championships, trophies, or statistics. Growth, consistency, leadership, teamwork, sportsmanship, perseverance, and service are also worth recognizing.

H.A.G. wants to help highlight the athletes, teams, coaches, and community members who are making a positive impact throughout the Heartland region.

Recognition submissions may be considered for website features, social media posts, community spotlights, event acknowledgments, or other H.A.G. communications.

What We Recognize

Achievements, growth, leadership, and community impact.

H.A.G. welcomes recognition submissions from families, coaches, teams, schools, clubs, organizations, businesses, and community members.

⭐

Athlete Achievements

Personal milestones, tournament performances, school sports accomplishments, awards, records, selections, and meaningful competitive moments.

🏆

Team Accomplishments

Championships, runner-up finishes, tournament results, league achievements, improvement, teamwork, and memorable team experiences.

📈

Growth & Development

Athletes who demonstrate significant improvement, perseverance, confidence, discipline, commitment, or dedication to their development.

📣

Coach Recognition

Coaches who teach, lead, mentor, communicate, develop athletes, build positive cultures, and represent their programs with integrity.

🤝

Sportsmanship & Leadership

Athletes, teams, and coaches who demonstrate respect, accountability, teamwork, leadership, and character during competition.

🌎

Community Impact

Volunteers, supporters, organizations, and businesses that help strengthen local athletics and create opportunities for athletes and families.

Submission Guidelines

Help us understand the story.

Recognition submissions should provide enough detail for H.A.G. to understand the accomplishment, growth, leadership, or community impact being recognized.

Please include accurate names, team or program information, the date or timeframe, and a clear description of what occurred.

Submitting information does not guarantee publication. H.A.G. may contact the person submitting the story for clarification, confirmation, permission, or additional information before sharing it publicly.

Submit Recognition

Helpful Submission Details

  • Name of the athlete, team, coach, or organization
  • Age, grade, team, school, club, or program when relevant
  • Description of the accomplishment or positive impact
  • Date, event, tournament, season, or timeframe
  • Contact information for follow-up questions
  • Confirmation that the information is accurate

Photos may be requested separately before a recognition feature is published.

Recognition Standards

Positive, accurate, and community-centered.

H.A.G. recognition is intended to celebrate meaningful stories while respecting athletes, families, teams, organizations, and the broader sports community.

✓

Accuracy

Names, results, accomplishments, dates, and other submitted details should be complete and accurate.

🛡️

Respect

Recognition should celebrate success without insulting, embarrassing, diminishing, or targeting another athlete, team, coach, official, or organization.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Appropriate Permission

H.A.G. may request parent, guardian, team, school, club, or organizational permission before publishing names, photos, or identifying information.

📋

Review

Submissions may be edited for clarity, length, accuracy, formatting, privacy, or consistency with H.A.G. communication standards.

⭐

Positive Impact

Recognition should highlight achievement, development, leadership, service, perseverance, teamwork, or another positive contribution.

🌎

Regional Connection

Priority may be given to stories connected to Quincy, the Tri-State area, and the broader Heartland region.

Recognition Process

What happens after you submit?

H.A.G. will review the information provided and determine whether the submission fits a future recognition opportunity.

We may follow up to verify details, request additional context, obtain appropriate permission, or ask for a suitable photo.

Timing may depend on the number of submissions, available content space, event schedules, and current H.A.G. programming.

Not Just Winning

Development deserves recognition too.

Some of the most meaningful sports stories involve athletes who remained committed, became better teammates, overcame challenges, learned new skills, showed leadership, or continued working when progress was difficult.

H.A.G. encourages submissions that recognize the process behind growth, not only the final result.
