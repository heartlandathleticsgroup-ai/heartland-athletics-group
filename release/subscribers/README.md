# Subscriber consent launch — review candidate

Independent from PR #28 and Gmail. Reuses the production consent ledger and original tested narrow TLS gateway driver. New email-only endpoint is disabled by default; no delivery code, Google authorization or SMS requests. The subscriber form is disabled and noindex until separately activated. Exact notice version hag-subscriber-notice-v1 maps to the existing internal consent version draft-3c3-v1; archive this commit and the exact published wording when activated. No confirmation/welcome email. New wrapper has bounded admission, but shared rate limit is not a bot-proof barrier and hosted availability/security and SQL lifecycle tests are required before opening enrollment. It trusts only the narrow database gateway role, not service_role. Gateway login remains NOLOGIN until privately provisioned, only one database URL and verified CA are required; no Gmail secrets.

Policy drafts require owner review and Illinois counsel review (not a guarantee of compliance). Confirm actual processor/cookie/log practices, retention/deletion procedure, support inbox coverage and age-audience classification/COPPA obligations for whole site. Legal basis: FTC CAN-SPAM guide https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business ; TCPA 47 CFR 64.1200 https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/64.1200 ; FCC Oct 26 2026 final rule https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-10-26/html/2026-21821.htm ; COPPA https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule-coppa ; Illinois PIPA https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext?DocName=081505300K45 ; carrier registration example https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/a2p-10dlc/collect-business-info .

Before email marketing: valid postal footer supplied privately (do not put home address on site), truthful sender/subject, appropriate commercial identification, working unsubscribe links for at least 30 days, opt-outs promptly and no later than 10 business days, provider limits and deliverability, tested suppression and verified preference link flow. Before SMS: choose provider/registered sender, approved campaign, independent written consent identifying HAG and number, explicit age and ownership confirmation, number verification, inbound STOP/HELP and reasonable revocations, channel suppression, reassigned-number handling, applicable quiet hours/DNC rules, fees and provider terms. No paid provider authorized or purchased. Do not infer consent from forms, rosters, sponsors or payments. No public links or policy effective dates until approval. Manual revoke RPC is staged, admin UI and production tests remain required; no campaigns before operational opt-out workflow works. No production changes in this draft.
