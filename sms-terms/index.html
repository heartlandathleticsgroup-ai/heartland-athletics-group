Heartland Athletics Group LLC SMS Messaging Terms

SMS is not available. These proposed terms describe a future program only and are not effective. Twilio is the selected provider; no texting number or program is active.

Program

Heartland Athletics Group LLC, serving the Quincy, Illinois and Tri-State region, will offer optional recurring automated marketing text messages to adults who expressly opt in and verify their mobile number. Messages may include selected H.A.G. news, Heartland Enforcers updates and tryouts, Gus Macker – Quincy event and registration updates, camps and clinics, tournaments, sponsorship and volunteer opportunities. SMS is separate from email; no inquiry or registration automatically enrolls you.

Consent

By affirmatively selecting the SMS consent box, providing a mobile number you are authorized to use and sending the unique, expiring JOIN code from that mobile number to our dedicated toll-free number, you agree to receive recurring automated marketing text messages from Heartland Athletics Group LLC about the sports and update types you select for SMS. Sport-specific marketing matches both interests. Selecting all current and future sports includes later-added sports; otherwise later-added sports are not automatically selected. Adding text interests requires renewed consent and confirmation when supported. Consent is not required to purchase, register or participate. You must be at least 18 years old. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consult your wireless plan for applicable charges.

Opt-out

Reply STOP to a message to unsubscribe from all H.A.G. marketing SMS. We will also honor recognized commands including QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL and UNSUBSCRIBE, and other reasonable revocation requests through our monitored contact channels. A nonmarketing confirmation of your opt-out may be sent where lawful. You may contact us directly to revoke consent. Opt-outs are honored promptly; no purchase, fee, login or new marketing consent is required. Sport and update-type preferences do not override a channel-wide opt-out. Email preferences are separate. START may remove a carrier-level messaging block but does not restore H.A.G. marketing consent. Rejoining requires fresh affirmative consent and verified re-enrollment; until the re-enrollment process is supported, contact H.A.G. for assistance.

Help and support

Reply HELP for assistance or contact Heartland Athletics Group LLC at info@heartlandathleticsgroup.com or (217) 577-0815. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Availability depends on supported carriers, devices and service coverage. Notify us if you change or relinquish your number.

Privacy

See H.A.G.'s Privacy Policy. We do not sell or share mobile opt-in information or consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Necessary service providers may process information on our behalf to deliver and administer messages. Verification, consent and suppression records are maintained to support your choices.

Changes

We may update these terms and the program, with notice as required. Any materially expanded marketing consent requires a new affirmative opt-in. No sending number or SMS program has been activated as of this review.
