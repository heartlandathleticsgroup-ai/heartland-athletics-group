SMS Messaging Terms

Program not active. H.A.G. does not currently accept SMS enrollment or send automated marketing texts. These proposed terms are for review before activation.

Program and written consent

Heartland Athletics Group LLC plans to offer optional recurring automated marketing text messages about the topics adults choose, including H.A.G. news, events, Gus Macker – Quincy, Enforcers, camps, clinics, sponsorship and volunteering. Before activation, you must provide a number you control, separately and affirmatively authorize texts from H.A.G., and verify the number. Consent is not a condition of purchase, registration or participation. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

When active, reply STOP to stop H.A.G. marketing texts or HELP for assistance. We will honor other reasonable revocation methods, including QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL and UNSUBSCRIBE, and direct requests at info@heartlandathleticsgroup.com. We intend to suppress the channel promptly. A lawful, nonpromotional opt-out confirmation may be sent. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Notify us when you relinquish a number. A new verified opt-in is required to rejoin after revocation.

Privacy and changes

See the Privacy Policy. Mobile consent and opt-in information will not be sold or shared for another party's marketing or promotional purposes. Necessary messaging processors operate on our behalf. Changes that materially expand consent require a new affirmative opt-in. Program terms, support and provider registration must be reviewed before activation.
